Partner FAQ

Questions before you apply

Is there a cost to join the partner program?
No. There is no fee to apply or to join the Debtrex partner program. You earn through per-lead payouts, revenue share, or volume-based pricing depending on your partner track.
How and when do partners get paid?
Approved partners are paid on a Net-15 cycle by ACH or wire once their first batch of leads clears the standard quality and return window. Your partner manager will walk you through the exact schedule during onboarding.
Do I need a license to become a partner?
It depends on your role and state. Referral and affiliate partners who only market and refer typically do not need a debt-specific license, but some states regulate debt-relief lead generation. Servicing partners must hold the licenses required to operate in their states. Our compliance team confirms requirements with you during the review step.
What marketing channels are allowed?
Compliant channels including content, search, social, email to opted-in lists, and display. We do not permit deceptive ads, fake government or law-firm branding, spam, or unsolicited robocalls and texts. All creative is reviewed before launch to protect both brands.
How quickly can I start sending leads?
Most partners complete onboarding within a week. Once your compliance review is approved and your tracking and assets are set up, you can go live immediately.
How are leads tracked and attributed?
Affiliate and referral partners receive unique tracking links, and where relevant, dedicated landing pages. Your dashboard shows every lead, its acceptance status, and earnings in near real time, so attribution is transparent.
What makes a lead "qualified"?
A qualified lead is a consumer who has given valid consent to be contacted and meets the baseline criteria for a debt relief consultation — generally a meaningful amount of unsecured debt, genuine financial hardship, and residence in a serviced state. Exact criteria are shared during onboarding.
Can I partner with Debtrex from outside the US?
The consumers we serve are based in the United States. International marketers and agencies are welcome to apply as long as campaigns target US consumers and follow US consumer-protection rules.
