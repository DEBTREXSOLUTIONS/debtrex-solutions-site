Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: January 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Debtrex Solutions LLC ("Debtrex," "we," "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your device and remember information about your visit. "Similar technologies" include pixels, web beacons, local storage, session storage, and software development kit (SDK) tools.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

Essential (always on)

These cookies are required for the site to function and cannot be disabled. They support core features such as page navigation, form submission, security, and remembering your consent preferences.

Analytics (opt-in)

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our site — for example, which pages are visited most and how long visitors stay. This information is aggregated and used to improve our content and user experience. We do not use analytics cookies to identify individual visitors.

Marketing & Advertising (opt-in / right to opt out)

These cookies are set by advertising platforms (notably Meta / Facebook) and allow us to measure the effectiveness of our ads and deliver more relevant advertising. Specifically, we use the Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API for ad measurement and attribution.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar state laws, our use of marketing/advertising cookies may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. California residents (and residents of states with similar laws) have the right to opt out of this sharing at any time.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences in three ways:

  • On our site: Use our Cookie Preferences panel at any time to update your choices.
  • "Do Not Sell or Share": Click Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information to immediately opt out of all marketing/analytics tracking.
  • Browser settings: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Some browsers also support the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal — when we detect a GPC signal, we automatically treat it as a valid opt-out request.
