Eligibility

Who may benefit from a consultation

Debt relief programs are not one-size-fits-all. The assessment helps determine whether a program may be a reasonable fit.

Typical profile that may qualify

  • $10,000 or more in unsecured debt (credit cards, personal loans, collections)
  • Experiencing genuine financial hardship
  • Struggling to keep up with minimum payments
  • Stable enough income to fund a structured program
  • Looking for an alternative to bankruptcy

When other options may be better

  • Mostly secured debt (mortgage, auto loans)
  • Under $10,000 in unsecured balances
  • Good credit and the ability to consolidate at a lower rate
  • No current hardship — just looking to optimize
  • Situations better handled by a bankruptcy attorney

FAQ

Common questions

Is Debtrex Solutions a law firm or government agency?
No. Debtrex Solutions is a debt relief consultation platform. We are not a law firm, we do not provide legal advice, and we are not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency. If you need legal advice, please consult a licensed attorney.
How much does the consultation cost?
The educational consultation is free. There is no upfront fee to learn about your options. If you choose to enroll in a debt relief program through one of our servicing partners, that partner will disclose all applicable fees in writing before you sign anything.
Will this affect my credit?
The assessment itself does not affect your credit — there is no credit pull. However, debt relief programs (including debt settlement) may negatively impact your credit during and after the program. A consultant will explain the credit implications of any program you consider so you can make an informed decision.
Can you guarantee how much I'll save?
No. Anyone who guarantees specific savings is not being honest with you. Outcomes vary by creditor, balance, account history, state, and individual circumstances. A consultant can explain typical ranges, but actual results cannot be guaranteed.
What types of debt qualify?
Debt relief programs generally focus on unsecured debts — credit cards, personal loans, medical bills, and some collections. Secured debts (such as mortgages and auto loans), federal student loans, tax debts, and child support are typically not eligible.
How quickly will someone contact me?
If you may qualify, a consultant typically reaches out within minutes to a few hours during business hours. You can also opt for a scheduled callback at a time that works for you.
What if I don't qualify?
If a program isn't a good fit, we'll tell you — and we'll suggest other resources that may help, such as nonprofit credit counseling, consumer education materials, or other options worth exploring.
